The Experience

Suya Night,
Any Night.

Suya is a night thing. You find the suya spot by the smoke and the smell before you find the stall itself: thin-sliced meat, hot off the grill, dusted in a spice mix perfected over generations.

That's the standard we hold ourselves to. Every skewer is hand-rolled in our own yaji, a nut-free blend of ginger, garlic, cayenne and warming spices, then grilled hard and fast so the outside chars while the inside stays juicy.

No pre-mixed seasoning from a packet, no shortcuts. Just the spice, the fire, and fresh onion and tomato to cut through the heat.

Our Story

Built On Yaji,
Not Shortcuts.

Suya isn't complicated. It's a handful of ingredients done properly: thin-cut meat, our own nut-free spice blend, the right ratio of heat to depth and a hot grill. KenSuya exists because too many places cut corners on all four.

We roast and grind our own yaji in-house, in small batches, so it's always fresh, not sitting in a tub for weeks losing its punch. Every skewer gets rolled by hand, not dunked.

We built this to taste like the suya spot you'd queue for back home. If it's quick, it's not suya, so we don't rush it. We just get faster at doing it right.
