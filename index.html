This Ain't Fast Food.
It's Slow Fire.

No conveyor belt, no heat lamps, no shortcuts. Just fire, patience, and a marinade that's been in the family longer than the shop has.

Our Story

Built From My Dad's
Back Garden.

It started with an oil drum, cut in half and welded into a grill by my dad in a garden two streets from here. Sunday afternoons, neighbours leaning over the fence asking what smelled so good.

By 2016 the drum had a market stall. By 2018, a shop front on the same road. The recipe hasn't changed — Scotch bonnet, allspice, thyme, and however long it takes to get it right.

We're not chasing trends. We're keeping a fire lit that's been in this family for three generations, one plate at a time.

"If it's quick, it's not jerk." — Marcus Blake, Founder

How We Cook

Built Around Fire.

No gas. No shortcuts. Every drum is fed with real pimento wood — the same wood jerk cooking has used for centuries. It's where the flavour actually comes from, not a bottle.

01. The Wood Pimento wood, imported and seasoned, burned down to the coal before anything touches the grate.
02. The Smoke Low, slow, indirect heat. A whole chicken can take up to two hours. We don't rush the drum.
03. The Char That black crust isn't burnt — it's flavour. Sugar and spice caramelising against real heat.
